## C. Semantic Review

### Does the theorem match the user's intent?

Partially — the user's ultimate intent is invertibility (D ∘ U = id). This theorem proves
the first necessary condition: injectivity of U on each circle when tangential distortion
is absent. The scope restriction is honest, documented in the file header, and consistent
with the work queue. The theorem is a meaningful step, not a proxy.

### Is the theorem vacuous?

No. Non-vacuity witness: take k all-zero, p all-zero, any two distinct ε₁ ≠ ε₂ with
`sensorRadius ε₁ = sensorRadius ε₂` (e.g., ε₁ = ⟨1, 0⟩, ε₂ = ⟨0, 1⟩, both at radius 1).
With all-zero k, R = 1 ≠ 0 (by `radial_zero_coefficients_identity`). U is then the
identity map, so U(ε₁) = ε₁ ≠ ε₂ = U(ε₂). The hypotheses hp1, hp2, hR, hSameR, h₁, h₂
are all satisfiable, and the conclusion `ε₁ = ε₂` is genuinely forced by `hU`.

### Are the hypotheses necessary?

- `hp1, hp2` (p = 0): necessary — with nonzero p, U on a circle is not a pure scaling,
  and injectivity may fail for pathological coefficients.
- `h₁, h₂` (denominatorNonzero): necessary — required by `radialTerm` and `undistortX/Y`.
- `hR` (R ≠ 0): necessary — if R = 0 then U maps the entire circle to the origin;
  distinct points have identical U outputs; injectivity fails.
- `hSameR` (same radius): necessary for this slice — equates R₁ = R₂ without needing
  monotonicity. Without it, the proof requires a separate argument (SLICE-UI-01).

### Load-bearing definition alignment

- `radialTerm k r h` ignores `h` in its body — confirmed by reading `RadialPolynomial.lean`.
  This is the critical property enabling `simp only [radialTerm, hSameR]` to close `hRR`
  without proof-irrelevance machinery.
- `undistortX`, `undistortY` definitions confirmed against paper Eq (16).
- `SensorPoint.ext` generated by `@[ext]` on `CoordinateTypes.lean`.

### Hard step: was it identified and correctly handled?

Yes. The hard step was `hRR` — equating the radial terms across different `h` arguments.
The proof used `simp only [radialTerm, hSameR]`, which unfolds the definition (dropping `h`)
and then equates the radius arguments. This worked cleanly. No proof-irrelevance manipulation
was needed because the definition already ignores `h`.

## C. Semantic Review

### Does the theorem match the user's intent?

Yes, for the stated scope. The user's goal is global injectivity of U for the pure-radial
case (p = 0). This theorem proves exactly that, given a caller-supplied hypothesis
`hScaleInj` asserting that r ↦ (R(r)·r)² is injective on [0,∞). The injectivity of the
radial scaling function is a genuine mathematical hypothesis that cannot be proved from
the model alone without coefficient conditions — `hScaleInj` correctly defers it to the
caller rather than asserting it vacuously.

### Is the theorem vacuous?

No. Non-vacuity witness: take k all-zero, p all-zero, ε₁ ≠ ε₂ distinct. With all-zero k,
`radialScale k r = 1` for all r, so `hScaleInj` requires `r₁² = r₂² → r₁ = r₂` on [0,∞),
which holds by `Real.sqrt_inj`. `hR₁` holds because radialTerm = 1 ≠ 0. U is the identity
map (all coefficients zero), so U(ε₁) ≠ U(ε₂) if ε₁ ≠ ε₂, meaning `hU` forces ε₁ = ε₂
(the hypotheses are jointly unsatisfiable unless ε₁ = ε₂ — precisely what the theorem claims).

### Are the hypotheses necessary?

- `hp1, hp2` (p = 0): necessary — with nonzero tangential the proof via squared radii fails.
- `h₁, h₂` (denominatorNonzero): necessary — required by radialTerm and undistortX/Y.
- `hR₁` (R(r₁) ≠ 0): necessary — if R = 0, U maps all of ℝ² to the origin; injectivity fails.
- `hScaleInj`: necessary — the theorem cannot be proved for all k without a monotonicity or injectivity assumption on the radial factor. Encoding it as a hypothesis is the correct choice: it defers the coefficient-condition analysis to SLICE-UI-02.

### Load-bearing definition alignment

- `radialScale k r` has the same body as `radialTerm k r _` (h ignored). The bridge lemma
  `radialTerm_eq_radialScale` makes this explicit and is proved by `simp [radialTerm, radialScale]`.
- `Real.sq_sqrt` is the Mathlib lemma `sqrt x ^ 2 = x` for `0 ≤ x`. Applied to
  `x = ε.x ^ 2 + ε.y ^ 2`; `positivity` closes the nonnegativity side condition.
- The reduction to UI-00 in the final step is direct: `sensorRadius ε₁ = sensorRadius ε₂`
  is produced from `hScaleInj`, which is exactly what `undistortPoint_injective_zero_tangential` requires.

### Hard step: was it identified and correctly handled?

The hard step was `hSumXY`: deriving `R₁² * (x₁² + y₁²) = R₂² * (x₂² + y₂²)` from
`hX2 : R₁² * x₁² = R₂² * x₂²` and `hY2 : R₁² * y₁² = R₂² * y₂²`. The tactic
`nlinarith [hX2, hY2]` closed it cleanly (add the equalities, distribute). No fallback needed.
